\begin{abstract}
This report details the computational annotation of a 10,000-row corpus of German immigration news for Economic Threat and Economic Benefit frames using an LLM-based approach. During prompt development, a 200-row pilot was used for iteration. The final, untouched 1,002-row human evaluation revealed that the LLM achieved high recall but low precision, resulting in F1 scores of 0.531 for Threat and 0.500 for Benefit. The model demonstrated an over-classification of positive frames. A key methodological limitation is that identical paired submitted coder files were used for reliability statistics, meaning file-level evidence cannot independently establish separate coding processes.
\end{abstract}
